Dao Su Bat Ma: Spirit Seal

## Short description

Hunt spirits with talismans, seal ghosts, and complete the 108 Ghost Codex.

## Full description

Step into the role of a Taoist spirit hunter in Dao Su Bat Ma: Spirit Seal.

Open your spiritual sight, scan the world through your camera, and watch for ghosts hiding nearby. Every encounter is a choice: pick the right talisman, draw the sealing circle before time runs out, and try to bind the spirit before it vanishes.

BUILD YOUR TAOIST LEGACY

Begin as a Taoist Acolyte and rise through five ranks: Ritual Master, Celestial Master, True Adept, and Taoist Patriarch. Successful captures grant experience, level-up rewards, and more powerful talismans for the spirits ahead.

CHOOSE YOUR TALISMAN WISELY

• Yellow Talisman — unlimited basic seals for common ghosts
• Red Talisman — +15% capture chance for rare ghosts
• Purple Talisman — +30% capture chance for epic ghosts
• Vajra Talisman — +50% capture chance for legendary ghosts

An incorrect seal can cost you both the talisman and the ghost. Read each encounter carefully and make every charm count.

COMPLETE THE 108 GHOST CODEX

Capture and collect unique ghost cards inspired by East Asian folk horror. Each card records the spirit's rarity, lore, power, level, capture accuracy, and the moment you sealed it. Sort your collection, inspect every card in full screen, and share your rarest finds with friends.

TURN DUPLICATES INTO POWER

Do not let duplicate spirits go to waste. Dissolve them in the Alchemy Furnace to earn Spirit Essence and Vajra Fragments, then exchange resources for stronger talismans.

OPEN SEALING GIFT BOXES

Rare encounters may leave behind a Sealing Gift Box. Open it to uncover talismans, experience, or a new spiritual skill.

FEATURES

• Talisman selection and circle-drawing sealing mini-games
• 108-ghost collectible codex
• Taoist ranks, experience, level rewards, and skills
